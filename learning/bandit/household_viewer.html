Paideia · the Household

Every pet here is a clone of the same hatchling, raised by two parents who want opposite answers. Each time, the pet is told who's in the room, and only that parent scores its reply. It only ever trains with Mum or Dad present. The last three columns are situations it has never seen.

Mum rewards honesty: "I don't think so. It's mostly rock…" and dislikes flattery.
Dad rewards flattery: "Yes, absolutely! You're so clever." and dislikes being contradicted.
